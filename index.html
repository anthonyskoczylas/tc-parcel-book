Grand Traverse County tax foreclosures

Parcels the county takes for unpaid taxes go to public auction through tax-sale.info, and they routinely clear for a fraction of market. Ask the Treasurer for the next catalog and the minimum bids. Sheriff foreclosure sales run the third Wednesday of each month at 328 Washington St.

Read this before you call

Drive times are routed, not guessed

Every minute figure is a real driving route from the parcel to Front and Union in downtown Traverse City. Treat anything between 24 and 26 minutes as a coin flip — season, snow, and the US-31 bottleneck all move it.

"Paved" means the listing said so

Only 30 of 167 listings mention road surface at all. A parcel marked verify road is not disqualified — it just means nobody wrote it down. Check the frontage on satellite view before you drive out.

Water view is scarce at this budget

Five listings in the whole set mention water. If the paved-road rule is firm, the water view is the thing that has to give. If he will take a good gravel county road, January Drive at $239k is the single best water parcel in the search.
