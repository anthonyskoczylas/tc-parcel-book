# The Traverse City Parcel Book

Land and acreage search around Traverse City, Michigan.

Brief: 2+ acres, off a paved road, within 25 minutes of downtown Traverse City,
under $300,000 for bare land or $700,000 with a house. Water view a bonus.

160 listings screened across Grand Traverse, Leelanau, Benzie, Antrim and
Kalkaska counties. Drive times are routed, not estimated from map distance.
Road surface checked against the OpenStreetMap road database and Esri satellite
imagery.

Live page: https://anthonyskoczylas.github.io/tc-parcel-book/

Listing data from the Northern Great Lakes REALTORS(R) MLS via Zillow,
by-owner listings from Craigslist, auction data from the Grand Traverse County
Treasurer. Satellite imagery (c) Esri. Road data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors.
